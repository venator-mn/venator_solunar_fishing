Major (moon overhead or underfoot) Minor (moonrise or moonset) Sunrise and sunset

Major periods are centered on the moon's upper and lower transit, and minor periods on moonrise and moonset. Rise and set times use the standard upper-limb convention with atmospheric refraction, matching the U.S. Naval Observatory method, and typically land within a minute or two of published tables. A prime note appears when a period overlaps the hour on either side of sunrise or sunset. Location search is provided by Open-Meteo using GeoNames data, and all of the timing math runs in your browser. The page address updates with each search, so you can bookmark a spot.
